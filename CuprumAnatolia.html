How we buy

From inquiry to payment

  1. Inquiry

    Tell us the grade, tonnage, packing and loading port. Add photos or a lab report if you have one.

  2. Offer

    We quote per tonne, priced against the LME with a discount that reflects grade and delivery terms.

  3. Inspection

    An independent inspector samples and weighs at loading. Disputes go to an agreed third-party lab.

  4. Payment

    Paid by LC at sight or TT against shipping documents. Final weights and assay set the final invoice.
